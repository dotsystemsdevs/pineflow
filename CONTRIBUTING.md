# Contributing

Pineflow is one exact path, so every change has to keep it exact.

## What helps most

- **A step that no longer works.** Tools change every month. Open an issue with the chapter, the step, what happened and your setup.
- **A dead or better link.** Videos and docs disappear. Suggest a replacement that shows exactly that step.
- **A fix you tested.** If you changed a command or a prompt and it worked on a fresh project, open a pull request with what you ran.

## House rules

- One tool per job. Pineflow doesn't list five alternatives for every step.
- Short sentences, plain words. Written for someone who has never opened a terminal.
- Every step ends with a check: what you see when it worked.
- No em dashes.
- Templates live in `templates/` with their real file names. Keep them in step with the chapter that uses them.

The chapters are also published at [dotsystems.dev/pineflow](https://dotsystems.dev/pineflow), where progress saves in your browser.
